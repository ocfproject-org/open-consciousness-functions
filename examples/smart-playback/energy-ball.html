    How Smart Playback works

    Full Guidance plays the original recording, including required steps. Experienced selects recommended shortcuts for this session. Advanced lets you choose the available sections individually. Open Settings next to Play to switch modes.

    Apply to this session keeps your defaults unchanged. Save as my defaults remembers choices on this browser. Basic command auditions remember their own choices separately from Energy Ball and Quiet Focus. Changing guidance pauses playback; press Play when ready. Reset clears saved Smart Playback defaults for all sessions and restores the default for this session.

    Options vary by recording. Focus, Quick Recharge, and Sleep Easy have no Energy Ball prep. Standard leaves Resonant Tuning and the main affirmation off. Use Advanced to turn either on; the affirmation is optional even when tuning is on. The Energy Ball lesson keeps both teaching cycles, Access, sleep reinforcement and the entire descent to Focus 10. Use Advanced to choose available options independently. Quiet Focus offers its own Energy Ball practice and short waking return. Both sessions use one count into Focus 10 after preparation; Short relaxation uses Plus Relax instead of another count. Downloads use the original recording for Energy Ball and Quiet Focus, and Standard guidance for the three basic commands. They do not follow session customizations.

    Locking the screen should not pause the page by itself. If an edited session cannot continue, your selected guidance stays in place. Retry that selection, or switch to Full Guidance yourself. Full Guidance uses the browser’s regular audio player.
